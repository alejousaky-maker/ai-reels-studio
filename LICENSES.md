## Content made by the app
- **Characters, environments, icons, music and SFX** are generated by code in this app (original drawings / procedural
  synthesis). Music and SFX are synthesized locally and are original.
- **Voices**: Kokoro voices (Apache-2.0). Imported voices must belong to you or have the speaker's explicit consent.
  No voice cloning is implemented.
- **Uploaded assets** are stored with the license/rights text you enter; the upload is refused without it.

## Before commercial use (checklist)
1. Remotion: confirm your organisation qualifies for the free license or buy a Company License (remotion.dev/license).
   Reselling or redistributing this app with Remotion inside is a separate question — read their terms.
2. Check the license of any Ollama model you use for scripts (model card).
3. If you redistribute the app (not just the videos), review GPL obligations for espeak-ng and FFmpeg builds.
4. Keep FACTS.md: claims marked `uncertain` must stay worded as uncertain; ads may only state client-approved claims.
5. Don't use logos, brands, people or footage you don't own or have permission for.
