# FACTS — Caesar vs the Pirates

Status: `verified` = checked against the listed public source; `uncertain` = reported/disputed, worded as such in the script; `fiction` = analogy or invented detail; `approved_by_client` = supplied by the client.

- **verified** — Around 75 BC Cilician pirates captured young Caesar and asked 20 talents; he said he was worth at least 50. ([source](Plutarch, Life of Caesar 1-2; https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Julius_Caesar))
- **verified** — He was held about 38 days, wrote poems and speeches, read them to the pirates, called them illiterate barbarians, and told them to be quiet when he slept. ([source](Plutarch, Life of Caesar 1-2; https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Julius_Caesar))
- **verified** — He jokingly threatened to punish them; after the ransom he raised a fleet without public office, captured them and had them executed. ([source](Plutarch, Life of Caesar 1-2; https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Julius_Caesar))

## Creative hooks (not claims)
- Pirates kidnapped Julius Caesar and asked for twenty talents. He was offended. He told them to ask for fifty.
