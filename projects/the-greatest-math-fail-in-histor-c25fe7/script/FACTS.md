# FACTS — The Greatest Math Fail in History

Status: `verified` = checked against the listed public source; `uncertain` = reported/disputed, worded as such in the script; `fiction` = analogy or invented detail; `approved_by_client` = supplied by the client.

- **verified** — Educated Europeans in 1492 knew the Earth was round; the flat-Earth belief is a later myth. ([source](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Myth_of_the_flat_Earth))
- **verified** — Earth's circumference was known to be roughly 25,000 miles (Eratosthenes-type estimates). ([source](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Christopher_Columbus))
- **verified** — Columbus took Alfraganus's degree in Arabic miles as Roman miles, underestimating Earth by about 25%. ([source](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Christopher_Columbus))
- **verified** — He placed Japan roughly 2,400 nautical miles west of the Canary Islands. ([source](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Christopher_Columbus))
- **verified** — Royal experts rejected his plan because his distance estimates were too small. ([source](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Christopher_Columbus))
- **verified** — He landed in the Bahamas in October 1492 and called the inhabitants 'indios'. ([source](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Christopher_Columbus))
- **verified** — In 1494 he had his crew swear that Cuba was part of the Asian mainland. ([source](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Christopher_Columbus))
