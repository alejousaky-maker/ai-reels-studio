# The Greatest Math Fail in History

Language: en · style: storybook · 14 beats · 156 words (~46 s at 3.4 words/s)

**1. [medium, golden, upbeat]** This guy was so bad at math, he accidentally found two continents.
   - _see:_ Columbus smug on the beach, his caravel rammed into the shore behind him.
**2. [close, golden, whimsical]** And he died refusing to admit he was lost.
**3. [medium, day, curious]** Here's the myth: in 1492, people thought the Earth was flat.
**4. [two_shot, day, upbeat]** Nope. Educated Europeans knew it was round, and roughly how big.
**5. [close, day, curious]** About twenty-five thousand miles around. Huge.
**6. [medium, day, curious]** Columbus read an Arabic measurement, and used shorter Roman miles instead.
**7. [close, day, upbeat]** Boom. His Earth shrank by a quarter.
**8. [medium, day, whimsical]** On his map, Japan sat just a few weeks west of the Canary Islands.
**9. [wide, day, tense]** The royal experts checked his math, and said no.
**10. [close, day, tense]** Not because of a flat Earth. Because his crew would starve in an empty ocean.
**11. [wide, golden, tense]** And they were right. Except two continents nobody in Europe knew about were in the way.
**12. [medium, day, whimsical]** He landed in the Bahamas, and called the people he met Indians.
**13. [medium, golden, whimsical]** Later, he made his crew swear that Cuba was part of Asia.
**14. [close, golden, warm]** The worst math in history, and the luckiest wrong answer ever.
