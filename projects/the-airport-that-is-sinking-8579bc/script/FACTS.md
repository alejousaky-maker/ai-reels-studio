# FACTS — The Airport That Is Sinking

Status: `verified` = checked against the listed public source; `uncertain` = reported/disputed, worded as such in the script; `fiction` = analogy or invented detail; `approved_by_client` = supplied by the client.

- **verified** — Kansai International Airport (opened 1994) sits on an artificial island about 5 km off the coast in Osaka Bay.
- **verified** — Fill came from cut-down mountains; the seabed holds ~18-20 m of soft alluvial clay.
- **verified** — The island has sunk about 11-12 m since construction, faster than predicted; terminal columns are adjustable with jacks.
- **verified** — Seawalls protect the island (Typhoon Jebi flooded it in 2018); Kansai is repeatedly ranked among the most punctual airports.
