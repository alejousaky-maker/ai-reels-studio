- **verified** — Polyphemus ate several of the men; Odysseus gave him strong unmixed wine and said his name was 'Nobody' (Outis). ([source](Homer, Odyssey IX))
- **verified** — They blinded his single eye with a heated olive-wood stake; the neighbours left when he cried that 'Nobody' was hurting him. ([source](Homer, Odyssey IX))
- **verified** — In the morning he moved the stone to let his flock out and the men escaped clinging under the rams. ([source](Homer, Odyssey IX))
