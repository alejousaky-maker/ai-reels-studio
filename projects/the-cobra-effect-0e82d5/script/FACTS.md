# FACTS — The Cobra Effect

Status: `verified` = checked against the listed public source; `uncertain` = reported/disputed, worded as such in the script; `fiction` = analogy or invented detail; `approved_by_client` = supplied by the client.

- **uncertain** — The 'cobra effect' (term coined by economist Horst Siebert) describes a bounty on cobras in colonial Delhi that led to cobra breeding; when the bounty ended, bred snakes were released. ([source](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Perverse_incentive (anecdote; historicity uncertain)))
- **fiction** — The story is an anecdote whose historicity is not established; it is narrated as 'the story goes'.
