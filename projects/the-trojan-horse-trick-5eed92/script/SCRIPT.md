# The Trojan Horse Trick

Language: en · style: storybook · 14 beats · 150 words (~44 s at 3.4 words/s)

**1. [wide, day, upbeat]** Ten years of war couldn't break one wall. So one guy weaponized FOMO.
   - _see:_ A Greek soldier's spear bounces off Troy's colossal wall; dizzy stars.
**2. [medium, day, whimsical]** Troy's walls were so strong, Greek spears bounced right off.
**3. [medium, day, curious]** So Odysseus drew a plan. A giant wooden horse. Hollow.
**4. [wide, day, upbeat]** His best soldiers hid inside, and the whole army sailed away.
**5. [close, day, whimsical]** Well, they pretended to. They hid behind an island.
**6. [medium, day, whimsical]** They left one guy behind on the beach. Sinon. Tied up. On purpose.
**7. [two_shot, day, curious]** The Trojans found him, and he started crying.
**8. [close, day, curious]** That horse is a gift for the goddess Athena, he sobbed.
**9. [medium, day, tense]** They built it huge, so you could never get it into your city.
**10. [two_shot, day, tense]** Because if it gets inside, Troy can never fall.
**11. [wide, day, upbeat]** The Trojans could not resist stealing the Greeks' luck.
**12. [wide, day, tense]** So they tore open their own unbreakable wall, to squeeze it in.
**13. [medium, night, tense]** That night, the Greeks climbed out, and opened the gates.
**14. [close, night, warm]** Ten years of attacks failed. Reverse psychology and a wooden toy didn't.
