# FACTS — The Trojan Horse Trick

Status: `verified` = checked against the listed public source; `uncertain` = reported/disputed, worded as such in the script; `fiction` = analogy or invented detail; `approved_by_client` = supplied by the client.

- **verified** — The Greeks besieged Troy for ten years. ([source](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Trojan_Horse))
- **verified** — Odysseus devised the hollow wooden horse; soldiers hid inside; the fleet sailed to hide behind the island of Tenedos. ([source](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Trojan_Horse))
- **verified** — Sinon was left behind and claimed the horse was an offering to Athena, built huge so Troy could not take it in, since inside it would protect the city. ([source](Virgil, Aeneid II))
- **verified** — The Trojans breached their own walls to bring the horse in; at night the Greeks came out and opened the gates. ([source](Virgil, Aeneid II))
- **fiction** — 'FOMO' and 'wooden toy' are comic framing.
