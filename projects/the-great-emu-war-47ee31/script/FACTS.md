# FACTS — The Great Emu War

Status: `verified` = checked against the listed public source; `uncertain` = reported/disputed, worded as such in the script; `fiction` = analogy or invented detail; `approved_by_client` = supplied by the client.

- **verified** — In Nov-Dec 1932 the Australian army (Maj. G.P.W. Meredith, two soldiers, two Lewis guns, 10,000 rounds) was sent against emus in Western Australia. ([source](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Emu_War))
- **verified** — About 20,000 emus were damaging wheat crops. ([source](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Emu_War))
- **verified** — At a dam ambush a gun jammed after about a dozen birds were killed; Meredith compared the emus' resilience to tanks. ([source](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Emu_War))
- **verified** — The operation was withdrawn, resumed, and averaged about 10 rounds per confirmed kill. ([source](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Emu_War))
- **fiction** — 'Lost a war to birds' is the popular comic framing.

## Creative hooks (not claims)
- In 1932, Australia sent soldiers with machine guns to fight birds. The birds won.
- A modern army. Machine guns. And the only war Australia ever lost to a bird.
