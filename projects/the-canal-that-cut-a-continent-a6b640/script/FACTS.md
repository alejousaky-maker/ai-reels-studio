# FACTS — The Canal That Cut a Continent

Status: `verified` = checked against the listed public source; `uncertain` = reported/disputed, worded as such in the script; `fiction` = analogy or invented detail; `approved_by_client` = supplied by the client.

- **verified** — The Panama Canal expansion (opened 2016) cost about $5.25 billion; Neopanamax ships pass with only a few metres to spare.
- **verified** — The canal opened in 1914; over 25,000 workers died across the French and American construction eras, mostly of disease.
- **verified** — New locks use gravity-fed water (with water-saving basins), not pumps; tugboats guide ships in the new locks.
- **verified** — About 40 percent of US container traffic passes through the canal; New York to San Francisco is ~8,000 miles shorter.
- **verified** — Droughts in 2023 forced draft limits; some ships paid millions in auctions to jump the queue.
