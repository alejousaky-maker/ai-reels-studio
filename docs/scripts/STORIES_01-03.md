# Shorts 01–03: scene-by-scene scripts (2.5D cel-shaded bean-stickman style)

60 s each, 14 scenes (cuts every ~4 s). The narration is written for an English TTS voice (af_heart); the spoken lines
below are exactly what the app voices. These scripts are the source of each video's `plan.json`.
Facts were checked against the sources listed at the end of each story.

---

## STORY 1: The Greatest Math Fail in History (Columbus, 1492)

**Scene 1 (0:00-0:04) - Wide**
- **Voiceover (EN):** "This guy was so bad at math, he accidentally found two continents." *(fast, deadpan, slight smirk on "accidentally")*
- **Visual Prompt:**
  - Golden-hour tropical beach: warm sand, turquoise water, low-poly palm trees, all with bold black ink outlines.
  - A wooden caravel with white sails and red crosses has rammed its bow into a palm tree.
  - In front of it stands the Columbus bean-stickman: a red explorer coat with gold buttons, a dark wide-brim hat, and a smug half-lidded smile. He holds a map upside down.
  - Warm low sun, long cast shadows. Camera at chest height, slightly low.
- **Action & Sound FX:** The palm shakes as the hull hits it and a cartoon impact burst pops at the bow; Columbus doesn't even look. *CRUNCH + coconut "bonk".*

**Scene 2 (0:04-0:07) - Close-up**
- **Voiceover (EN):** "And he died refusing to admit he was lost." *(dry, beat of silence after "lost")*
- **Visual Prompt:**
  - Columbus close-up: proud raised brows, closed-eye smile, a tiny rolled map in hand.
  - Soft blurred palms behind him; warm rim light.
- **Action & Sound FX:** He lifts his chin and a question mark pops above his head. *Ding.*

**Scene 3 (0:07-0:11) - Medium**
- **Voiceover (EN):** "Here's the myth: in 1492, people thought the Earth was flat." *(storyteller, mock-serious)*
- **Visual Prompt:**
  - A stone village square in daylight.
  - A villager bean-stickman in a brown tunic looks worried while a scroll floats above him.
  - Cobblestones and houses with ink outlines, soft background blur.
- **Action & Sound FX:** The scroll pops in on "myth". *Paper flutter.*

**Scene 4 (0:11-0:15) - Two-shot**
- **Voiceover (EN):** "Nope. Educated Europeans knew it was round, and roughly how big." *(quick correction, playful)*
- **Visual Prompt:**
  - A scholar bean-stickman (grey hair, purple robe) holds a book and a small globe.
  - The villager reacts with surprise. Warm daylight.
- **Action & Sound FX:** A lightbulb pops over the scholar on "round". *Pop!*

**Scene 5 (0:15-0:19) - Close-up**
- **Voiceover (EN):** "About twenty-five thousand miles around. Huge." *(slow on "huge")*
- **Visual Prompt:**
  - The scholar smiles knowingly.
  - On-screen label "≈ 25,000 miles". Shallow depth of field.
- **Action & Sound FX:** The label slides in. *Whoosh.*

**Scene 6 (0:19-0:23) - Medium**
- **Voiceover (EN):** "Columbus read an Arabic measurement and used shorter Roman miles instead." *(conspiratorial, faster)*
- **Visual Prompt:**
  - Inside a warm wooden study: shelves, window light, a desk.
  - Columbus stands with a book, confident, one eyebrow up.
- **Action & Sound FX:** A book icon floats up on "Arabic"; Columbus nods. *Page flip.*

**Scene 7 (0:23-0:27) - Close-up**
- **Voiceover (EN):** "Boom. His Earth shrank by a quarter." *(punch on "Boom")*
- **Visual Prompt:**
  - Columbus grins with closed "happy" eyes.
  - A comic impact burst behind his head; label "−25 % Earth".
- **Action & Sound FX:** A burst pops on "Boom". *Cartoon BOOM + shrinking "zwip".*

**Scene 8 (0:27-0:31) - Medium**
- **Voiceover (EN):** "On his map, Japan sat just a few weeks west of the Canary Islands." *(matter-of-fact, then incredulous)*
- **Visual Prompt:**
  - Columbus proudly holds his map toward the camera in the study.
  - A tiny globe the size of a basketball floats beside him.
- **Action & Sound FX:** The globe pops in on "Japan". *Ding.*

**Scene 9 (0:31-0:35) - Wide**
- **Voiceover (EN):** "The royal experts checked his math and said no." *(stern)*
- **Visual Prompt:**
  - A royal throne room: red carpet, golden throne, banners, warm window light.
  - A crowned king bean-stickman (gold crown, red robe) sits sternly on the throne; Columbus stands before him.
- **Action & Sound FX:** The king frowns and a scroll pops up on "no". *Stamp THUD.*

**Scene 10 (0:35-0:39) - Close-up**
- **Voiceover (EN):** "Not because of a flat Earth. Because his crew would starve in an empty ocean." *(dry, logical)*
- **Visual Prompt:**
  - The crowned king close-up: stern, one eyebrow raised.
  - A clock icon floats beside him.
- **Action & Sound FX:** The clock pops in on "starve". *Ticking.*

**Scene 11 (0:39-0:44) - Wide**
- **Voiceover (EN):** "And they were right. Except two continents nobody in Europe knew about were in the way." *(build-up, then surprise)*
- **Visual Prompt:**
  - Open ocean at golden hour. The caravel sails on gentle low-poly waves.
  - Sailors on the deck look nervous.
- **Action & Sound FX:** A big exclamation mark pops on "Except". *Seagull + wave whoosh.*

**Scene 12 (0:44-0:49) - Medium**
- **Voiceover (EN):** "He landed in the Bahamas and called the people he met 'Indians.'" *(light, ironic)*
- **Visual Prompt:**
  - On the beach, Columbus waves proudly.
  - Two local islanders in simple light clothing look at him, puzzled. Palms and turquoise water.
- **Action & Sound FX:** A question mark pops over an islander on "Indians". *Ding.*

**Scene 13 (0:49-0:54) - Medium**
- **Voiceover (EN):** "Later, he made his crew swear that Cuba was part of Asia." *(incredulous)*
- **Visual Prompt:**
  - Evening on the beach. Columbus raises a scroll; two sailors stand with nervous faces.
  - Warm sunset light, long shadows.
- **Action & Sound FX:** The scroll pops on "swear". *Quill scratch.*

**Scene 14 (0:54-1:00) - Close-up**
- **Voiceover (EN):** "The worst math in history, and the luckiest wrong answer ever." *(warm, punchline, loops to the hook)*
- **Visual Prompt:**
  - Columbus close-up at sunset, holding the upside-down map, smiling smugly as stars twinkle above his head.
- **Action & Sound FX:** He winks and the stars sparkle. *Sparkle + soft musical sting.*

*Sources:*
- [Christopher Columbus](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Christopher_Columbus): his geographical errors, the rejection by experts, and the 1494 oath on Cuba.
- [Myth of the flat Earth](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Myth_of_the_flat_Earth).
- Columbus used Alfraganus's degree (Arabic miles) as if it were in Roman miles: an Earth about 25 % too small.

## STORY 2: The Trojan Horse (Reverse Psychology vs. an Unbreakable Wall)

**Scene 1 (0:00-0:04) - Wide**
- **Voiceover (EN):** "Ten years of war couldn't break one wall. So one guy weaponized FOMO." *(fast, deadpan)*
- **Visual Prompt:**
  - A sandy plain in front of a colossal stone city wall with towers.
  - A Greek bean-stickman in bronze armor and a red-crested helmet throws a spear; it bounces off the wall like a toothpick.
  - Bright day, hard shadows, ink outlines on every brick.
- **Action & Sound FX:** The spear hits the wall, then an impact burst and dizzy stars over the soldier. *TINK + boing.*

**Scene 2 (0:04-0:08) - Medium**
- **Voiceover (EN):** "Troy's walls were so strong, Greek spears bounced right off." *(mock-awe)*
- **Visual Prompt:**
  - Two Greek soldiers stand in front of the wall, one with dizzy spiral eyes.
  - The wall fills the background.
- **Action & Sound FX:** A puff of dust rises at the wall base. *Thud.*

**Scene 3 (0:08-0:12) - Medium**
- **Voiceover (EN):** "So Odysseus drew a plan. A giant wooden horse. Hollow." *(sly, slower on "Hollow")*
- **Visual Prompt:**
  - The Greek camp on the beach.
  - Odysseus (bronze armor, crested helmet, beard) stands beside a wooden chalkboard with a badly drawn horse sketch.
- **Action & Sound FX:** A lightbulb pops over Odysseus on "plan". *Chalk squeak + pop.*

**Scene 4 (0:12-0:16) - Wide**
- **Voiceover (EN):** "His best soldiers hid inside, and the whole army sailed away." *(brisk)*
- **Visual Prompt:**
  - The finished giant wooden horse on wheels stands on the beach in warm light.
  - A Greek ship with a square sail is at the shoreline.
- **Action & Sound FX:** The soldiers march past the horse. *Wood creak + waves.*

**Scene 5 (0:16-0:20) - Close-up**
- **Voiceover (EN):** "Well, they pretended to. They hid behind an island." *(whisper-aside)*
- **Visual Prompt:**
  - Odysseus close-up winks, with his finger-to-lips energy. Soft blurred beach behind.
- **Action & Sound FX:** A sparkle on the wink. *Ding.*

**Scene 6 (0:20-0:24) - Medium**
- **Voiceover (EN):** "They left one guy behind on the beach. Sinon. Tied up. On purpose." *(comic timing, pauses)*
- **Visual Prompt:**
  - Sinon, in a ragged tunic, stands tied with rope bands around his bean body.
  - He looks nervous, sweat drop and all. The horse looms behind.
- **Action & Sound FX:** An exclamation mark pops on "purpose". *Rope creak.*

**Scene 7 (0:24-0:28) - Two-shot**
- **Voiceover (EN):** "The Trojans found him, and he started crying." *(fake-sad)*
- **Visual Prompt:**
  - A Trojan soldier (blue crest) leans toward Sinon, who wails with his eyes squeezed shut.
- **Action & Sound FX:** Sinon's face crumples on "crying". *Fake sob.*

**Scene 8 (0:28-0:32) - Close-up**
- **Voiceover (EN):** "'That horse is a gift for the goddess Athena,' he sobbed." *(dramatic)*
- **Visual Prompt:**
  - Sinon close-up, teary and pleading. A scroll floats beside him.
- **Action & Sound FX:** The scroll pops on "gift". *Harp gliss.*

**Scene 9 (0:32-0:36) - Close-up**
- **Voiceover (EN):** "'They built it huge, so you could never get it into your city.'" *(conspiratorial)*
- **Visual Prompt:**
  - Sinon looks sideways and nervously. The horse is blurred behind him.
- **Action & Sound FX:** An exclamation mark pops on "never". *Sting.*

**Scene 10 (0:36-0:40) - Medium**
- **Voiceover (EN):** "'Because if it gets inside, Troy can never fall.'" *(whisper, then loud)*
- **Visual Prompt:**
  - The Trojan soldiers' eyes widen with greedy grins. A lightbulb appears over one of them.
- **Action & Sound FX:** The lightbulb pops on "inside". *Pop.*

**Scene 11 (0:40-0:44) - Wide**
- **Voiceover (EN):** "The Trojans could not resist stealing the Greeks' luck." *(fast)*
- **Visual Prompt:**
  - Trojans push the wooden horse across the plain toward the wall, cheering.
- **Action & Sound FX:** Wheels roll. *Rumble + cheering.*

**Scene 12 (0:44-0:49) - Wide**
- **Voiceover (EN):** "So they tore open their own unbreakable wall to squeeze it in." *(incredulous)*
- **Visual Prompt:**
  - The great wall now has a broken gap with rubble at its base.
  - The horse stands in the gap; dust puffs rise.
- **Action & Sound FX:** Stones crash and dust bursts. *CRUMBLE.*

**Scene 13 (0:49-0:54) - Medium**
- **Voiceover (EN):** "That night, the Greeks climbed out and opened the gates." *(low, ominous)*
- **Visual Prompt:**
  - Night, deep blue. Greek soldiers stand next to the horse inside the walls; lanterns glow.
- **Action & Sound FX:** A creak, then a burst on "gates". *Creak + BOOM.*

**Scene 14 (0:54-1:00) - Close-up**
- **Voiceover (EN):** "Ten years of attacks failed. Reverse psychology and a wooden toy didn't." *(punchline, loops to the hook)*
- **Visual Prompt:**
  - Odysseus close-up at night, smug grin. Stars sparkle.
- **Action & Sound FX:** He grins and sparkles pop. *Sparkle + sting.*

*Sources:*
- [Trojan Horse](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Trojan_Horse).
- Virgil, *Aeneid* II: Sinon's story, the horse as an offering to Minerva/Athena, and the Trojans breaching their own walls. The episode is not in the *Iliad*.

## STORY 3: How "Nobody" Outsmarted a Giant (Odyssey, Book 9)

**Scene 1 (0:00-0:04) - Medium**
- **Voiceover (EN):** "This guy escaped being eaten alive with the worst dad joke in history." *(fast, deadpan)*
- **Visual Prompt:**
  - A dark cave lit by a crackling fire.
  - A giant one-eyed Cyclops bean-stickman, three times human size, in a brown fur tunic, towers over two tiny terrified Greek stickmen.
  - Warm firelight from below, deep shadows, ink outlines on rocks.
- **Action & Sound FX:** The giant looms closer and the tiny men shake. *Low rumble + gulp.*

**Scene 2 (0:04-0:08) - Wide**
- **Voiceover (EN):** "Odysseus and his men were trapped in a cave with a giant Cyclops." *(storyteller)*
- **Visual Prompt:**
  - Wide shot of the cave: a huge round boulder seals the entrance and sheep stand around.
  - Odysseus (bearded, crested helmet) and his crew are small in the foreground.
- **Action & Sound FX:** Sheep bleat. *Baa + fire crackle.*

**Scene 3 (0:08-0:12) - Close-up**
- **Voiceover (EN):** "He blocked the door with a boulder, and started eating them." *(grim-comic)*
- **Visual Prompt:**
  - The Cyclops close-up: a single huge eye and a toothy grin. The boulder is behind him.
- **Action & Sound FX:** An impact burst on "boulder". *Rock THUD.*

**Scene 4 (0:12-0:16) - Medium**
- **Voiceover (EN):** "Easy fix: kill him while he sleeps, right?" *(mock-casual)*
- **Visual Prompt:**
  - A crewman whispers to Odysseus, eager. The sleeping giant is in the background.
- **Action & Sound FX:** A question mark pops on "right". *Ding.*

**Scene 5 (0:16-0:20) - Medium**
- **Voiceover (EN):** "Wrong. Only the giant could move that boulder. Kill him, and you starve inside." *(sharp correction)*
- **Visual Prompt:**
  - Odysseus points at the huge boulder blocking the cave mouth. His crewman's face drops.
- **Action & Sound FX:** An exclamation mark pops on "Wrong". *Record scratch.*

**Scene 6 (0:20-0:24) - Two-shot**
- **Voiceover (EN):** "So Odysseus offered him wine. Strong wine. No water." *(sly)*
- **Visual Prompt:**
  - Odysseus offers a clay wine jug up to the towering Cyclops, who grins.
- **Action & Sound FX:** The wine icon pops on "wine". *Slosh.*

**Scene 7 (0:24-0:28) - Close-up**
- **Voiceover (EN):** "The giant asked his name. 'My name,' he said, 'is Nobody.'" *(slow, deadpan on "Nobody")*
- **Visual Prompt:**
  - Odysseus close-up, perfectly straight face, one eyebrow up. Firelight flickers.
- **Action & Sound FX:** A beat of silence before "Nobody". *Fire crackle.*

**Scene 8 (0:28-0:31) - Medium**
- **Voiceover (EN):** "Then the Cyclops passed out." *(quick)*
- **Visual Prompt:**
  - The Cyclops sways with dizzy spiral eyes, then slumps. Stars circle above.
- **Action & Sound FX:** Dizzy stars spin. *Snore.*

**Scene 9 (0:31-0:36) - Medium**
- **Voiceover (EN):** "They heated a sharpened olive stake in the fire, and drove it into his one eye." *(tense, fast)*
- **Visual Prompt:**
  - Odysseus and his crew carry a long glowing olive-wood stake.
  - The giant is offscreen in shadow. A big cartoon impact burst — no gore.
- **Action & Sound FX:** A burst flashes on "eye". *Sizzle + giant ROAR.*

**Scene 10 (0:36-0:40) - Wide**
- **Voiceover (EN):** "He screamed for his Cyclops neighbors. 'Who's hurting you?'" *(echoing)*
- **Visual Prompt:**
  - Outside the cave at night: a rocky hillside under the moon. Two other one-eyed giants call toward the boulder-sealed entrance.
- **Action & Sound FX:** A question mark pops over a neighbor. *Echoing shout.*

**Scene 11 (0:40-0:43) - Close-up**
- **Voiceover (EN):** "'Nobody is killing me!'" *(loud, panicked)*
- **Visual Prompt:**
  - The blinded Cyclops close-up in pain, his single eye squeezed shut. Burst lines.
- **Action & Sound FX:** Shock burst. *Giant wail.*

**Scene 12 (0:43-0:48) - Two-shot**
- **Voiceover (EN):** "'Well, if nobody's hurting you, go back to sleep.'" *(bored neighbor voice)*
- **Visual Prompt:**
  - The two neighbor giants shrug at each other at night and turn away.
- **Action & Sound FX:** They walk off. *Crickets.*

**Scene 13 (0:48-0:54) - Wide**
- **Voiceover (EN):** "At dawn, the blind giant rolled the boulder away himself, and the men slipped out under his sheep." *(rising relief)*
- **Visual Prompt:**
  - Golden dawn at the cave mouth, with the boulder rolled aside.
  - Sheep trot out, and the Greeks walk out among them.
- **Action & Sound FX:** The boulder rumbles and sheep bleat. *Rumble + baa.*

**Scene 14 (0:54-1:00) - Close-up**
- **Voiceover (EN):** "Saved by a fake name. Nobody saw that coming." *(punchline, loops to the hook)*
- **Visual Prompt:**
  - Odysseus close-up at sunrise, smug grin. A sparkle.
- **Action & Sound FX:** He grins and sparkles pop. *Ding + sting.*

*Sources:*
- [Polyphemus](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Polyphemus).
- Homer, *Odyssey* IX: unmixed wine, "Outis / Nobody", the olive stake, the neighbors' reply, and the escape under the rams.
