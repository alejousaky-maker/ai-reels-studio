# Shorts 04–06: scene-by-scene scripts (2.5D cel-shaded bean-stickman style)

These are fictional "caught cheating" stories (comedy dramatizations, not real events), 60 s each, 14 scenes.
The narration is for the English TTS voice (af_heart); the spoken lines below are exactly what the app voices.

Change made for Story 2: the "living-room security footage" became the synced messages and the doorbell camera clips
(the boss arriving at the house). The twist works the same without anyone spreading private, intimate recordings.

---

## STORY 4: The Smart Scale Trap

**Scene 1 (0:00-0:04) - Wide**
- **Voiceover (EN):** "A guy caught his girlfriend cheating, because his bathroom floor snitched on her at 3 AM." *(fast, deadpan)*
- **Visual Prompt:**
  - A modern bathroom: white tiles, a glass shower with a curtain, warm vanity light, black ink outlines everywhere.
  - A smug bean-stickman in a sharp navy suit and red tie stands on a glowing Wi-Fi bathroom scale. A long red "BUSTED" receipt prints out of it.
- **Action & Sound FX:** The receipt shoots out and a red exclamation pops. *Printer zip + ding.*

**Scene 2 (0:04-0:07) - Medium**
- **Voiceover (EN):** "He was away on a business trip." *(matter-of-fact)*
- **Visual Prompt:**
  - An airport terminal: big windows with a plane outside, a departure board, rows of seats.
  - The suit guy walks with a suitcase.
- **Action & Sound FX:** He walks across the frame. *Rolling wheels + PA chime.*

**Scene 3 (0:07-0:11) - Medium**
- **Voiceover (EN):** "His girlfriend texted: sleeping alone all weekend. Miss you." *(sing-song, fake-sweet)*
- **Visual Prompt:**
  - A modern apartment living room: hardwood floor, sofa, TV, plant, city window.
  - The girlfriend (pink dress, long hair) smiles sweetly at her glowing phone. A heart pops.
- **Action & Sound FX:** A heart icon pops on "Miss". *Text whoosh.*

**Scene 4 (0:11-0:15) - Close-up**
- **Voiceover (EN):** "She forgot one tiny detail. His smart scale." *(sly)*
- **Visual Prompt:**
  - Bathroom close-up on the guy's grin. The glowing scale sits on the floor beside him; a Wi-Fi icon is above.
- **Action & Sound FX:** The Wi-Fi icon pulses. *Ding.*

**Scene 5 (0:15-0:19) - Medium**
- **Voiceover (EN):** "It syncs every single weigh-in to his phone." *(explaining, quick)*
- **Visual Prompt:**
  - A hotel room at night: bed, a neon sign glowing pink.
  - The guy sits with his phone, which glows.
- **Action & Sound FX:** A phone icon pops on "phone". *Notification blip.*

**Scene 6 (0:19-0:23) - Close-up**
- **Voiceover (EN):** "At 2:45 AM, his phone dinged." *(slow, ominous)*
- **Visual Prompt:**
  - The guy close-up at night, his face lit by the screen, eyes wide.
- **Action & Sound FX:** A clock icon pops on "dinged". *Ding!*

**Scene 7 (0:23-0:27) - Close-up**
- **Voiceover (EN):** "New weight recorded. One hundred ninety-five pounds." *(robot-flat)*
- **Visual Prompt:**
  - Close-up of the guy, deadpan. On-screen label "NEW WEIGHT: 195 lbs".
- **Action & Sound FX:** The label slams in. *Record scratch.*

**Scene 8 (0:27-0:31) - Medium**
- **Voiceover (EN):** "His girlfriend weighs one hundred fifteen." *(dry)*
- **Visual Prompt:**
  - In the apartment, the girlfriend freezes mid-sip, with a sweat drop. Label "SHE: 115 lbs".
- **Action & Sound FX:** Her face drops on "fifteen". *Gulp.*

**Scene 9 (0:31-0:36) - Medium**
- **Voiceover (EN):** "At 8 AM, the scale saved a brand new guest profile. With body fat." *(escalating)*
- **Visual Prompt:**
  - The bathroom in morning light, the scale glowing. A second stickman in a towel freezes in the shower doorway.
  - Label "GUEST PROFILE SAVED".
- **Action & Sound FX:** An exclamation mark pops on "guest". *Camera shutter.*

**Scene 10 (0:36-0:39) - Close-up**
- **Voiceover (EN):** "He didn't call. He didn't yell." *(calm, cold)*
- **Visual Prompt:**
  - The guy close-up in the hotel, eyes half-lidded, perfectly calm.
- **Action & Sound FX:** Silence, then a slow push-in. *Low hum.*

**Scene 11 (0:39-0:44) - Medium**
- **Voiceover (EN):** "He renamed the home Wi-Fi: Who weighs one ninety-five?" *(savage)*
- **Visual Prompt:**
  - In the living room, the girlfriend and the towel guy stare at a phone, shocked.
  - Label "WI-FI: WHO WEIGHS 195?"
- **Action & Sound FX:** A Wi-Fi icon pops and both faces turn to shock. *Ding + gasp.*

**Scene 12 (0:44-0:49) - Wide**
- **Voiceover (EN):** "Then he shipped all her stuff to her parents' house." *(brisk)*
- **Visual Prompt:**
  - A sunny suburban street: a stack of moving boxes and a delivery van in a driveway.
- **Action & Sound FX:** The van drives off. *Engine + thud.*

**Scene 13 (0:49-0:53) - Wide**
- **Voiceover (EN):** "Before his flight even landed." *(quick)*
- **Visual Prompt:**
  - The airport at golden hour. The guy strolls out with his suitcase, smiling.
- **Action & Sound FX:** He walks. *Plane whoosh.*

**Scene 14 (0:53-0:58) - Close-up**
- **Voiceover (EN):** "Turns out, cheating is hard when even the floor keeps receipts." *(cold punchline, loops to the hook)*
- **Visual Prompt:**
  - The guy close-up, smug, holding the "BUSTED" receipt. Sparkle.
- **Action & Sound FX:** A sparkle on "receipts". *Ding.*

## STORY 5: The Promotion Loophole
