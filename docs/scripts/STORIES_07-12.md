# Shorts 07–12: scene-by-scene scripts (2.5D cel-shaded bean-stickman style)

60 s each, 13 scenes (about 4.5 s per cut), 130–150 words of voiceover.

- **Part 1 (07–09)** is history, checked against sources. The Cobra Effect is told as the anecdote it is ("the story goes"); its exact numbers are not documented, so none are invented.
- **Part 2 (10–12)** is fiction (comedy dramatizations, not real events). Story 12 uses a hotel-hallway camera photo, never private or intimate footage.

These are scripts only; no video is produced from them unless requested.
