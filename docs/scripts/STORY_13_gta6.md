# Short 13: The Day the Earth Stood Still (GTA 6 release day)

Comedy fiction, 60 s, 13 scenes, voice af_heart. The game and console are named in the narration only; on screen they are generic,
unbranded props (no logos or trademarked art).

**Scene 1 (0:00-0:04) - Medium**
- **Voiceover (EN):** "I woke up this morning and thought the zombie apocalypse finally happened. Turns out, it was something way more serious." *(fast, deadpan, ominous on "serious")*
- **3D Environment & Lighting:** A sunny low-poly bedroom: warm wood floor, a soft morning sunbeam from the window on the right, long black-outlined shadows.
- **Character & Props (Cel-Shaded):** A bean-stickman in striped blue pajamas sits up in bed with "> <" sleepy eyes and his arms stretched up.
- **Action & SFX:** A big yawn freezes mid-stretch and he glances at the window. *Yawn → dead silence → a single crow caw.*

**Scene 2 (0:04-0:08) - Close-up**
- **Voiceover (EN):** "No traffic. No dogs. No neighbors yelling. Nothing." *(whisper, slowing down)*
- **3D Environment & Lighting:** Close on his face, with the bedroom blurred behind.
- **Character & Props (Cel-Shaded):** One eyebrow up, wide eyes, a small sweat drop.
- **Action & SFX:** His eyes dart left and right. *Clock tick, tick.*

**Scene 3 (0:08-0:12) - Wide**
- **Voiceover (EN):** "I opened the blinds. The street was completely empty." *(uneasy)*
- **3D Environment & Lighting:** A low-poly suburban street: clean asphalt, a traffic light blinking yellow, houses with lawns. Bright, flat noon light.
- **Character & Props (Cel-Shaded):** The street has zero cars and zero people. A brown 3D tumbleweed with ink outlines rolls across.
- **Action & SFX:** The tumbleweed bounces past. *Wind howl + tumbleweed scrape.*

**Scene 4 (0:12-0:16) - Medium**
- **Voiceover (EN):** "So I went out in my slippers to find another human being." *(determined, comic)*
- **3D Environment & Lighting:** A brick-storefront sidewalk with warm light and long shadows.
- **Character & Props (Cel-Shaded):** The pajama stickman shuffles along in fuzzy slippers, holding a coffee mug.
- **Action & SFX:** Slow shuffle steps. *Shff, shff.*

**Scene 5 (0:16-0:20) - Medium**
- **Voiceover (EN):** "The coffee shop? Closed. The bank? Closed. Even the grocery store." *(rising panic)*
- **3D Environment & Lighting:** Shops with metal roll-down shutters and neon signs.
- **Character & Props (Cel-Shaded):** Every door carries a "CLOSED UNTIL FURTHER NOTICE" sign; he stares at it, sweating.
- **Action & SFX:** Shutters clang one after another. *CLANG, CLANG, CLANG.*

**Scene 6 (0:20-0:24) - Close-up**
- **Voiceover (EN):** "That's when I really started to panic." *(high-pitched)*
- **3D Environment & Lighting:** The empty street, blurred.
- **Character & Props (Cel-Shaded):** Shock eyes, a giant sweat drop, an exclamation mark.
- **Action & SFX:** His face drops. *Record scratch.*

**Scene 7 (0:24-0:29) - Close-up**
- **Voiceover (EN):** "Then I checked my phone. November nineteenth." *(beat… dawning realization)*
- **3D Environment & Lighting:** Back in the bedroom, the phone glowing red in his hand.
- **Character & Props (Cel-Shaded):** The phone screen reads "NOV 19 — RELEASE DAY".
- **Action & SFX:** The notification pops. *DING.*

**Scene 8 (0:29-0:33) - Close-up**
- **Voiceover (EN):** "GTA 6 release day." *(reverent whisper)*
- **3D Environment & Lighting:** A warm glow with sparkles.
- **Character & Props (Cel-Shaded):** His 2D eyes turn into glowing stars and a huge grin spreads.
- **Action & SFX:** Sparkles burst around him. *Angelic choir "aaah".*

**Scene 9 (0:33-0:37) - Medium**
- **Voiceover (EN):** "The whole world wasn't gone. It was just… loading." *(punch on "loading")*
- **3D Environment & Lighting:** Living room windows; every house across the street flickers with the same blue TV glow.
- **Character & Props (Cel-Shaded):** He points out of the window with a lightbulb above his head.
- **Action & SFX:** Blue flickers. *Low synth hum.*

**Scene 10 (0:37-0:41) - Close-up**
- **Voiceover (EN):** "Curtains closed. Console on." *(fast, mission-mode)*
- **3D Environment & Lighting:** The curtains slam shut and the room drops into dark mode, lit by neon blue.
- **Character & Props (Cel-Shaded):** A sleek white-and-black console with bold ink outlines; his mitten hand presses the power button and it glows blue.
- **Action & SFX:** The curtains swoosh and the console powers up. *SWOOSH + BEEP.*

**Scene 11 (0:41-0:46) - Wide**
- **Voiceover (EN):** "Headset on. Snacks stocked. Phone on airplane mode." *(listing, quick)*
- **3D Environment & Lighting:** A gaming corner with an RGB strip glow and a gaming chair.
- **Character & Props (Cel-Shaded):** He is now in a hoodie with a 3D headset, flanked by a stack of pizza boxes and energy cans.
- **Action & SFX:** He settles in. *Can crack + pizza box thud.*

**Scene 12 (0:46-0:53) - Wide**
- **Voiceover (EN):** "Day one. Day three. Day seven." *(time-lapse, accelerating)*
- **3D Environment & Lighting:** Through the window, the sun and moon spin around and day and night flash.
- **Character & Props (Cel-Shaded):** He sits frozen in the chair: "@ @" bloodshot spiral eyes, a huge grin, the pizza boxes piling higher.
- **Action & SFX:** Rapid day-night flicker. *Ticking clock, sped up.*

**Scene 13 (0:53-0:59) - Close-up**
- **Voiceover (EN):** "The apocalypse was real. We just did it to ourselves, one mission at a time." *(dry punchline)*
- **3D Environment & Lighting:** The neon-blue room under the screen glow.
- **Character & Props (Cel-Shaded):** A bloodshot, unblinking stare, a slow grin, a sparkle on the headset.
- **Action & SFX:** One blink, finally. *Ding + controller click.*
